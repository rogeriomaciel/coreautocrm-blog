Bulk generic WhatsApp message blasts have become a major threat to customer retention in the automotive repair industry. Beyond annoying drivers with irrelevant promotional deals, spamming on WhatsApp frequently leads to permanent bans of official shop phone numbers.

However, letting pending repair estimates go cold on the front desk without follow-up is leaving significant revenue behind. To resolve this dilemma, **CoreAutoCRM** introduced **AI-Assisted Follow-up Generation**.

---

## Mass Spam Blasts vs. Front-Desk Follow-up Bottlenecks

Auto repair shops typically fall into one of two dangerous extremes:

1. **Mass Message Blasts (Spam):** The shop manager hires a tool to blast the exact same promo message ("Come in for a wheel alignment deal!") to 2,000 contacts. The result is a sub-1% response rate and frequent phone number bans.
2. **Neglected Leads (Lack of Time):** Service advisors are busy handling walk-in customers and simply lack the time to re-read past conversations to follow up on pending estimates.

**CoreAutoCRM** eliminates both extremes through a smart, humanized approach.

---
