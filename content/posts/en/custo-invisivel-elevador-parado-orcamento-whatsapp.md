## What Is the "Cost of an Idle Lift"?

In modern automotive management, your hydraulic lifts and alignment racks are your primary revenue-generating units. Every hour a lift sits occupied without active work being performed represents an **irrecoverable opportunity cost**.

If a lift has a potential revenue rate of **$350.00 per hour** combining labor and parts margin, keeping a car stuck on it for 4 hours awaiting approval means **losing $1,400.00 in unrecoverable revenue**.

## Unlocking Estimates and Freeing Up Lifts

To eliminate the hidden cost of idle lifts, high-performing shops adopt three practical measures:

### 1. Structured WhatsApp Communication
Instead of sending a cold PDF file or a confusing text price list, estimates should be presented with visual clarity, highlighting **immediate safety items** (brakes, tires) separately from **preventive maintenance**.

### 2. Active Recovery of "Leads in Limbo"
If a customer doesn't respond within 45 minutes of receiving an estimate, the system triggers the automated WhatsApp follow-up queue. An AI assistant sends a friendly check-in asking if they have questions about parts options or payment plans.

### 3. Technician Workflows Decoupled from Front Desk Sales
While the front desk works on securing customer approval on WhatsApp, technicians shouldn't sit idle. Using the **Clean Technician View**, technicians view active ROs in the queue by license plate and can move ahead with diagnostics on another vehicle in an open bay without getting distracted by sales negotiations.
