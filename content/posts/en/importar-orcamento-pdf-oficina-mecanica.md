## How Intelligent PDF Reading Works in Practice

Unlike legacy optical character recognition (OCR) tools that failed over misaligned text, domain-trained artificial intelligence understands the context of an automotive repair estimate:

1. **Structured Extraction:** The system parses the document layout, isolating customer details, vehicle specs (plate, model, year), and itemized repair quotes.
2. **Duplication Prevention:** If the vehicle or customer profile already exists, the system updates the existing record rather than creating duplicates.
3. **Active Work Order Supplementing:** If the vehicle is already on the hydraulic lift and the customer forwards a supplementary parts quote, new items append directly to the active Work Order.
