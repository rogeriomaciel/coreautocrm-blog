Managing the technical team in an auto repair shop is one of the most delicate tasks for a business owner. If Repair Order (RO) distribution feels informal or unfair, accusations of favoritism quickly arise. If there are no clear weekly delivery and revenue goals, shop productivity slows down, and customer promised times begin to slip.

The solution isn't excessive pressure or micro-managing technicians at their lifts—it's creating a **transparent visual system of Weekly Production Sprints**.

Inside **CoreAutoCRM**, the **Sprint & Rounds** module replaces uncertainty with a weekly goal dashboard where the entire team sees shop objectives and celebrates shared results.

---

## 1. The Concept of Weekly Production Sprints

Instead of only looking at monthly totals—which often leads to frantic rushing in the last 3 days of the month—the shop operates in **Weekly Sprints (Monday through Friday)**.

Under this model, the main dashboard highlights three key performance indicators for the entire team:

1. **Weekly Revenue:** Real-time accumulated revenue tracked against target goals (e.g., **$26,915.90 earned toward a $30,000.00 goal**).
2. **Delivered Repair Orders:** The number of vehicles completed and returned to customers compared to sprint targets (e.g., **19 out of 25 vehicles delivered**).
3. **Technician of the Week:** Visual recognition for the technician with the highest delivery pace and revenue generated (e.g., *Sidley with 4.4 ROs/day delivered and $12,500.00 in revenue*).

## 2. Individual Metrics: Team Health & Performance

To avoid generalized complaints, the sprint dashboard features the **Technician Health & Performance** panel.

Each technician profile monitors:
* Active Repair Orders currently assigned in their bays.
* Total ROs completed in the current sprint.
* Revenue generated by the technician.
* Capacity load percentage relative to maximum bay capacity.

This removes any perception of favoritism. If one technician has 5 active cars while another has only 1, management immediately identifies the imbalance on screen and redistributes ROs to prevent bottlenecks in a single bay.

---

## 3. Continuous Shop Floor Kanban

A major flaw in traditional auto repair software is mixing customers asking questions on WhatsApp with cars physically disassembled on the shop floor.

The **Sprint Kanban** solves this clutter by focusing strictly on **physical shop floor flow**:

* **Pits & Diagnostics:** Vehicles that just entered for technical inspection and OBD2 scanner testing.
* **In Maintenance:** Cars with approved estimates undergoing active work on brakes, suspension, engine, or oil service.
* **Waiting on Parts:** Vehicles temporarily paused pending auto part deliveries.
* **Completed:** Vehicles finished, washed, and ready for customer pickup.

All earlier stages (like *Triage*, *Pre-RO*, or *Leads in negotiation*) remain in the WhatsApp commercial funnel, ensuring the shop floor Kanban reflects 100% physical reality.

---

## 4. Impact on Shop Culture

When technicians see that shop progress is measured transparently and top performers are recognized with real data, team culture shifts dramatically:

* **Focus on Closing:** Technicians collaborate to move cars from *Waiting on Parts* to *Completed*.
* **Shared Celebration:** At the end of the sprint, the **"Close & Celebrate Sprint"** feature archives weekly history and logs achievements and key takeaways for the next cycle.
* **Financial Clarity:** The shop owner knows exactly what the team produced and how close they are to break-even and profit targets.
