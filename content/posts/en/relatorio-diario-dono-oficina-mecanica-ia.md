## What Is the Daily Executive Action Report?

Rather than requiring shop owners to navigate multiple software screens to hunt down bottlenecks, the **Virtual AI Board** operates as a digital C-suite auditing your business 24/7:

* **Virtual CFO:** Audits 7-day revenue, real average ticket size (separating parts from labor), and tracks liquidity locked in pending estimates.
* **Virtual Operations Director (PM):** Monitors hydraulic lift occupancy and alerts if a technician accumulates too many active jobs, preventing delivery delays.
* **Virtual CMO:** Tracks expired WhatsApp quotes and suggests customer re-engagement copy.
* **Virtual CEO:** Synthesizes all operational metrics into a concise **Top 3 Priority Action Plan** delivered straight to the owner's WhatsApp.
